# Reviewed New York HUT resources for partner websites

These links point to substantive carrier guidance on NewYorkHUT.com. A partner can choose a resource relevant to its audience and use its own description. Placement and any outreach require the partner's agreement. No outreach has been sent.

| Audience question | Resource | Suggested plain anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Does this truck need HUT? | https://newyorkhut.com/learn/who-needs-a-new-york-hut-permit | Check New York HUT permit requirements |
| How does a carrier register? | https://newyorkhut.com/learn/how-to-register-for-new-york-hut | New York HUT registration checklist |
| Is an occasional trip covered? | https://newyorkhut.com/guides/get-trip-certificate | New York HUT trip certificate guide |
| Where does the sticker go? | https://newyorkhut.com/learn/new-york-hut-decals-explained | HUT decal placement and replacement |
| What are the fees and taxes? | https://newyorkhut.com/cost | Understand New York HUT costs |
| What is due after the quarter? | https://newyorkhut.com/mt-903-filing-center | Prepare New York MT-903 filing records |

For a trucking-insurance agent's resource page, eligibility and registration are the first useful links. For a carrier/accounting resource page, filing and cost guides are more relevant. Keep the description factual and avoid claims of government affiliation, guaranteed exemption, rankings, or an open renewal window. Paid registration and filing actions are linked contextually inside the guidance to NYHUT.com.
