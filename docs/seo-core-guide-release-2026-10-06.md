# NewYorkHUT core guides and reviewed consolidation — October 6, 2026

This release improves five core topics across six existing URLs and consolidates 28 overlapping pages. The destination URLs and titles stay in place. No NYHUT.com transaction or account code changes.

## Source verification

Requirements were checked against the following New York Department of Taxation and Finance documents on October 6, 2026. Original bulletin issue dates remain distinct from our source-check date.

- TB-HU-40: introduction, weight definitions and separate credentials.
- TB-HU-115: regular registration, state fee, TMT-39/TMT-1 and TR-8.
- TB-HU-116: trip certificates, carrier-wide annual limit, expiry, cargo, fee and covered-period treatment.
- TB-HU-160: decals and vehicle-specific documents.
- TB-HU-245: excluded and exempt uses.
- TB-HU-260: filing requirements and reclassification.
- TB-HU-360 and HUT Schedule 1: weight methods and illustrated rates.
- TB-HU-765: source records, retention and consent for records outside New York.
- Current MT-903 instructions: weekends/holidays, no-tax, amended and final returns.
- Current TMT-39 and TMT-1 application forms.

Examples are illustrative, and organization authorship describes the real publishing site. No invented individual expert, testimonial or customer experience is asserted.
